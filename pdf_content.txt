FINAL YEAR PROJECT PROPOSALMEDIMIND AI: Powered Smart medication reminder for patient1.Introduction:MediMind AI: Powered Smart Medication Reminder for Patients is designed to help people remember their medicines on time. Many patients forget their doses because of busy schedules or managing multiple prescriptions, which can affect their health.MediMind AI solves this problem by sending clear reminders, organizing medication schedules, and keeping track of what the patient has taken. The system learns the user’s routine and makes the reminders more helpful and personalized. The goal of this project is to create an easy-to-use tool that supports patients in taking their medicines regularly and staying healthier.2.Objective:“To create a smart app that reminds patients to take their medicines on time and helps them keep track of their doses.”1.Send Timely Reminders: Help patients remember to take their medicines on time using smart alerts.2.Track Medications: Make it easy for patients to see which medicines they have taken and which are still pending.3.Personalize Schedules: Adjust reminders based on the patient’s routine and prescription timings.4.Reduce Mistakes: Lower the chances of missed or wrong doses and improve treatment effectiveness.5.Promote Better Health: Support consistent medicine intake, leading to healthier outcomes and a better quality of life.3.Problem description:
Many people forget to take their medicines on time, especially if they have busy lives, multiple prescriptions, or chronic illnesses. Missing doses or taking the wrong medicine can slow recovery, reduce treatment effectiveness, and sometimes cause serious health problems. Regular reminders like phone alarms or written notes are not always reliable and do not adapt to each person’s routine.MediMind AI is designed to fix this problem. It sends smart reminders, keeps track of medicine schedules, and helps patients stay on top of their medications. This makes it easier for patients to take their medicines correctly and stay healthier for better life.This system helps patients stay on track with their medications, reduces mistakes, and supports better overall health. While designing it, factors like easy-to-use interface, flexible reminders, accurate notifications, and privacy need to be considered.4:Methodology:The development of MediMind AI: Powered Smart Medication Reminder for Patients will follow a clear, step-by-step approach to make the system practical and easy to use:1.Understanding Requirements: Identify the problems patients face in taking medicines on time and determine what features the app should have.2.Designing the System: Plan the app’s structure, including the interface, database, and AI reminder system, making sure it is simple and reliable.3.Development: Build the app with features like personalized reminders, medicine tracking, and smart notifications that adjust to the user’s routine.4.Testing: Check that the app works correctly, reminders are accurate, and it is easy for patients to use.5.Deployment: Launch the app so real users can start managing their medications efficiently.6.Feedback and Improvement: Collect feedback from users to fix issues, improve features, and make the system even more helpful.•Third-Party Libraries / Tools:●For Mobile Development:○Flutter or React Native for cross-platform app development.○Firebase for real-time database, user authentication, and cloud notifications.●For AI / Smart Reminders:○Python libraries: scikit-learn for basic ML models, pandas and numpy for data handling.
○TensorFlow Lite if lightweight on-device AI predictions are needed.●Notification Libraries:○OneSignal or Flutter Local Notifications for sending timely alerts to users.●Database / Storage:○SQLite or Firebase Firestore for storing medication schedules and user data securely.•Techniques:●Personalized Scheduling: Adjust reminder times based on patient patterns and adherence history.●Data Validation & Error Handling: Ensure accurate medicine intake logging and notifications.●User-Friendly Design: Simple UI/UX for all age groups to improve engagement.5.Project Scope:The scope of MediMind AI: Powered Smart Medication Reminder for Patients includes the development of a digital system that helps patients manage their medications efficiently. The project focuses on:1.Medication Reminders: Sending timely,2. AI-powered notifications to ensure patients take their medicines on time.3.Personalized Scheduling: Adjusting reminders according to each patient’s routine and prescription requirements.4.Tracking and History: Recording medicines taken and pending doses for easy monitoring.5.User-Friendly Interface: Designing a simple and intuitive interface suitable for all age groups.6.Alerts for Caregivers: Optional notifications for family members or caregivers to support patients when needed.7.Data Security: Ensuring patient data is stored securely and privacy is maintained.8.Cross-Platform Access: Developing the app for mobile devices (Android/iOS) and potentially a web platform.Out of Scope: The project does not include direct integration with hospitals, doctors, or pharmacies, nor does it provide automated prescription refills.The system is intended to improve medication adherence, reduce errors, and enhance overall health management for patients, making medicine management easier and more reliable.
